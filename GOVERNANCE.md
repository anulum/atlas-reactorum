# Governance

## Ownership

Atlas Reactorum is owned and maintained by Miroslav Šotek (ANULUM / Fortis
Studio). It sits in the SCPN reactor-family portfolio as a standalone evidence
library and is cross-linked with, but not owned by, the reactor device
projects.

## Decision making

The owner is the final authority on scope, inclusion and release. Changes that
alter what the atlas *claims* — coverage boundaries, evidence tiers, the
separation of claim from result — require the owner's explicit agreement, not
merely a passing build.

## Evidence authority

No contributor, automated or human, may:

- present a company claim as an independently established result;
- infer reactor type, vessel count, fuel cycle, capacity, location or status
  from adjacent facts;
- import a bulk source without recording publisher, licence, retrieval date,
  selection rule and coordinate semantics;
- alter a source value to make a check pass.

A record that cannot be traced to a retrievable source does not enter the
atlas. "No independently supported result found" is a finding, not an absence.

## Change process

Every change passes the gates listed in `CONTRIBUTING.md` before landing.
The coverage floor is 100 per cent and is never lowered. A gate that cannot
execute is a failure, never a pass.

## Succession

Should maintenance transfer, the successor inherits these constraints. The
evidence discipline is the project; the code is how it is enforced.
