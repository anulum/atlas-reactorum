# Support

## Using the atlas

Open `04_interactive_presentation/index.html` in a modern browser. It is
static, needs no build step and no network, and works from `file://`.

Pan by dragging or with the arrow keys; zoom with the wheel or `+`/`-`;
reset with `0`; select a cluster to zoom into it. Every mapped record is also
reachable by keyboard and assistive technology.

## Questions

- **A record looks wrong.** Open a bug report and cite the retrievable source
  that contradicts it. Corrections without a source cannot be accepted.
- **Coverage is missing.** Open a coverage request with candidate sources and
  their licence terms.
- **Commercial licensing.** protoscience@anulum.li; see
  `COMMERCIAL-LICENCE.md`.
- **Security.** See `SECURITY.md`. Never a public issue.

## What this project will not do

It will not assert that a technology works, rank vendors, or estimate a
facility's capability beyond what its sources state. Those judgements are the
reader's, which is why claim, maturity and evidence are kept in separate
fields.
