# Discovery audit deliverables

Metadata-only audit current to **2026-09-26**. No completed section catalog was modified and no large publication was downloaded.

- `candidates.tsv` — 68 deduplicated bibliographic/data candidates: 27 nuclear/fusion, 21 chemical/biochemical, 14 hybrid/emerging, and 6 cross-cutting discovery services.
- `fusion_companies_candidates.tsv` — 51 company/project leads spanning active, qualified-active, newly announced/stealth, renamed/acquired, dormant, closed, and uncertain-status programs.
- `gaps.md` — prioritised family, literature, data, safety, and speculative-technology gaps.
- `methodology.md` — sources, queries, endpoints, evidence rules, deduplication, and limitations.

Important: inclusion is not endorsement. Corporate milestones and schedules are treated as self-reported unless explicitly identified as independently observed. Experimental fusion events, neutron production, funding, patents, and device construction do not by themselves demonstrate scientific breakeven, engineering gain, net electricity, or economic viability. Exhaustive completeness cannot be guaranteed.
