# Taxonomy corrections applied after the 123-entry audit

Applied on 2026-09-26 to `04_interactive_presentation/data/taxonomy-expanded.js`.

## Source corrections

- Replaced the unrelated IAEA accelerator-decommissioning publication with IAEA *Fusion Physics*, STI/PUB/1562, for the broad fusion reference.
- Replaced the unrelated Z-pinch DOI with Sandia's Z-pinch ICF hohlraum publication page.
- Replaced the unrelated compact-helical-system DOI with LLNL spheromak/SSPX coverage.
- Replaced the broken DOE electrolysis-target URL with the 2024 DOE water-electrolysis technology assessment.

## Classification corrections

- Updated pebble-bed HTGR maturity to deployed/established with the audit's HTR-PM qualification.
- Updated sodium fast reactor maturity to deployed/established while retaining design-specific caveats.

## Added high-priority coverage

Ten sourced entries were added, producing a 133-entry working index:

- pool, tank and tank-in-pool research-reactor geometries;
- aqueous homogeneous reactor;
- critical/subcritical assembly and pulsed research-reactor mode;
- chemical-looping system and sequencing batch reactor;
- upflow anaerobic sludge-blanket and fixed-film anaerobic reactors.

The audit remains a snapshot of the original 123 entries. The regenerated `taxonomy-expanded.sources.tsv` is the current machine-readable index. Per-claim page/section citation work, historical maturity facets and remaining medium-priority architectures are still open.

## Subcritical-assembly wording — 1 October 2026

The maintained entry now cites [IAEA-TECDOC-1976](https://www-pub.iaea.org/MTCD/Publications/PDF/TE-1976web.pdf),
section 2.1, printed page 3 (PDF page 13). Subcritical operation does not imply
that every assembly is structurally incapable of criticality. The blanket
reactivity-safety claim was removed; design-specific controls remain necessary.
The original audit rows and their dates are preserved. This correction does
not complete every claim's source review or establish an individual design's
safety.
