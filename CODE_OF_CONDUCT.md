# Code of conduct

## Standard

Participate professionally and in good faith. Address the work, not the person.
Disagreement about evidence is the normal state of a research project and is
settled by citing sources, not by assertion or by seniority.

Unacceptable: personal attacks, harassment, sustained disruption, publishing
others' private information, and deliberately misrepresenting a source.

## Evidence conduct

This project has one domain-specific rule beyond the usual: **do not overstate
what a source establishes.** Presenting a company claim, a funding round, a
patent or a rendering as a demonstrated technical result is a conduct matter
here, not merely an error, because the atlas exists to keep those apart.

## Reporting

Report concerns to protoscience@anulum.li. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

The owner may warn, revert contributions, or withdraw participation rights.
Decisions are recorded with their reasoning.
