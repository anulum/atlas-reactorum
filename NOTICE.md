## Third-party material

The atlas **catalogues** external datasets and literature. That material is not
covered by this project's licence and is not relicensed by inclusion here.

Source attribution and terms are recorded in each layer's registries, rights
notes and REUSE mappings; available per-record fields depend on the source and
record schema. The `source_publisher` and `source_license` fields are present
where that source contract supplies them. A citation URL does not license its
target. Publisher material outside a redistribution grant remains catalogue-only.

The 146-record historical FusionBenchmark / Hadamard LLC selection retains six
compilation/classification fields under the dated CC BY 4.0 permission, with
original normalisation indicated. Separate original Wikidata values are excluded
from the public table. Full original historical inputs require an explicit
external frozen bundle, whose hashes establish custody rather than redistribution
rights. Enrichments and the eleven primary-source additions have separate terms.
See [the historical input contract](05_global_reactor_map/imports/fusion/README.md).

Official datasets referenced under their own terms include PRIS, RRDB, FusDIS,
ARIS, INIS, ENDF, EXFOR, FENDL, ALADDIN, SANIS, ICSBEP and IRPhE. None was
scraped or bulk-redistributed. Consult
`00_index/COVERAGE_LEDGER.md` and each layer's rights note before reusing any
catalogued material.

Distributed facility data retain the combined source terms recorded in
[the data rights map](04_interactive_presentation/data/source_rights.json) and
[attribution](04_interactive_presentation/ATTRIBUTION.md). AGPL covers original
Atlas code and editorial contributions; it does not supersede foreign data
licences or grant rights to cited publisher works.
