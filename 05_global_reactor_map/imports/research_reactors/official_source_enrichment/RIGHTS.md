# Primary research source rights and attribution

This bundle retains the complete original 172-record research catalogue and
adds an Atlas-authored ledger for its 358 originally blank operator, purpose
and first-criticality fields. Original catalogue and earlier enrichment cells
retain their existing source terms. Atlas editorial scope and decision text
use the repository's AGPL-3.0-or-later terms; these terms grant no rights to
cited publisher works.

The ledger selects 275 isolated factual assertions and retains 83 individual
held or no-fill outcomes. A selection is bounded to its recorded historical
unit, operating institution, configuration and event precision. It is not a
claim that every facility is operational or independently verified. Unknown
means the cited inspected evidence does not establish the field; it does not
mean the fact is unknowable. Planned, subcritical, never-critical, relocated
and composite identities remain distinct.

[source_registry.tsv](source_registry.tsv) binds every cited source to its
original anonymous HTTPS URL, exact captured-body SHA-256, capture date,
retained rights notice and actual selected/held/no-fill counts. The ledger
records document dates separately from reactor events and capture dates.
Original PDFs, HTML payloads, scans, images, personal working paths and
acquisition logs are not distributed. Neither citation nor a hash grants a
redistribution licence for the underlying work.

CEA monographs retain their publisher rights. Their reproduction condition
requires Editorial Board agreement and source acknowledgement; no such
agreement is claimed here and the monographs are not embedded. IAEA and
national-institute publications likewise remain source references with their
own rights. An institution's domain does not imply public-domain status.
The registry preserves source-specific notices without assigning a new open
licence to all cited documents or relicensing original foreign source cells.

This is an original selection of short factual assertions and attribution,
not a bulk copy of RRDB or another publisher database. Consult the original
publisher before reusing its expression, images, document or database. The
separate historical FusionBenchmark input contract and its dated permission
are unaffected by this research-reactor bundle.
