# Rights and redistribution notes

## Brazil — Empresa de Pesquisa Energética

The official EPE Webmap page links the agency's downloadable geospatial energy system, including biofuel production units. The three imported ArcGIS layers are served directly from EPE's `epe.gov.br` infrastructure. EPE's official open-data page states that all content on the site is published under the **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International** licence.

Reuse basis: copy, redistribute and adapt with attribution under CC BY 4.0. This round attributes EPE in every row, preserves links to the official landing page and raw service, and identifies the transformation as field selection and schema normalisation. No endorsement is implied.

Suggested attribution:

> Contains data from Empresa de Pesquisa Energética (EPE), WEBMAP EPE biofuel plant layers, retrieved 2026-09-28, licensed under CC BY 4.0. Modified by field selection and schema normalisation.

Evidence:

- `https://www.epe.gov.br/pt/publicacoes-dados-abertos/publicacoes/webmap-epe`
- `https://www.epe.gov.br/pt/publicacoes-dados-abertos/dados-abertos`
- `https://gisepeprd2.epe.gov.br/arcgis/rest/services/SMA/Webmap_EPE_Biocombustiveis/MapServer`

## United States — EPA Landfill Methane Outreach Program

The Data.gov record published by the U.S. EPA Landfill Methane Outreach Program assigns **CC0 1.0** to the “Landfill Gas Energy Projects and Landfills in LMOP Database” dataset and marks it public. The associated ArcGIS item states that it is licensed under the US EPA Data Licensing Agreement for public use. The official EPA LMOP pages identify the map as an official presentation of LMOP database projects.

Reuse basis: CC0/public-use government data. Although attribution is not required by CC0, this round attributes EPA and LMOP in every row and retains direct links to the official database, map and service.

Suggested attribution:

> U.S. EPA Landfill Methane Outreach Program (LMOP), operational landfill-gas energy project map, September 2024 data release, retrieved 2026-09-28. CC0 1.0 / U.S. EPA public-use data. Modified by field selection and schema normalisation.

Evidence:

- `https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/landfill-gas-energy-projects-and-landfills-in-lmop-database`
- `https://www.epa.gov/lmop/lmop-national-map`
- `https://services.arcgis.com/cJ9YHowT8TU7DUyn/arcgis/rest/services/LMOP_Projects/FeatureServer/0`

## Boundary of this rights review

The rights conclusion applies to the two imported publishers and the exact layers listed in `source_snapshot_manifest.tsv`. A source merely appearing in `source_gap_matrix.tsv` is not approval to redistribute it. Backlog and deferred sources require their own data-model, rights and coordinate review before import.
