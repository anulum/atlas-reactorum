# Roadmap

Dated, forward-only. Items move to `CHANGELOG.md` when they land.

## Near term

- The default fusion map now uses a pinned IAEA FFDB catalogue with 174 source
  identities and 137 publisher map points. Its offline producer, complete
  field provenance, main integration and native browser checks are implemented.
  Retain absent fields and source/physical-identity distinctions while
  qualifying the explicit data-only selection and its source-specific rights.
  The full viewer and permission-page containers remain privately preserved.

- The structural taxonomy review of 2026-09-29 applied five findings. All 135
  entries carry historical classification and source-scope audit records:
  the merged critical/subcritical entry was split into two physically distinct
  entries, the two cross-assigned sources were reassigned to the entries they
  actually document, the beam-target neutron generator's evidence scope now
  states that it makes no net-energy claim, the aqueous homogeneous reactor
  records its historical operation and fuel-state axis, and the tank-in-pool
  geometry is marked as a combination of two parents rather than a third
  independent principle. Claim-level citations now supply 599 source-inspected
  statements for all 135 entries; 480 statements bind all 405 physical fields.
  Complete entry review and source-supported maturity/evidence classification
  remain pending. The [citation contract](metadata/taxonomy_audit/README.md)
  records the current coverage and each statement's support boundary.
- Company depth round 8 now has ten reviewed profiles, a 33-source registry,
  field associations, generated overlays and a native validator. Its
  [evidence boundary](metadata/company_audit/depth_round8/README.md) retains
  unresolved technical claims and prototype fuel rather than asserting
  physical gap closure. Continue source-specific technical verification.
- Industrial expansion round 7 supplies 421 source-bound observations from
  Swiss SFOE biogas and Italian ARPAE bioenergy sources. Its seven-artifact
  bundle, native validator and map consumer preserve original observations
  and separate source licences. Facility records do not establish vessel
  counts, reactor designs or current operation. Continue jurisdiction-specific
  source and facility-boundary reconciliation.
- Close research-reactor enrichment gaps: 96 operator, 117 purpose and 145
  first-criticality values, by exact-identifier overlay from official sources.

Source-supported facility fields currently retain 1,631 assertions for 1,342
purpose/use records and 533 fuel/feed-classification records. These source
categories leave reactor composition and physical operation unresolved;
additional source-specific coverage remains necessary.

## Medium term

- Extend source-supported purpose/process and fuel/feed coverage in the integrated facility
  schema, only where the upstream source actually supplies them. The present
  missing-field counts deliberately expose that weakness rather than hiding it.
- Reconcile power-reactor operator and type gaps against national regulators.
- Deepen chemical-reactor literature for multiphase, photobioreactor,
  electrochemical stack, dynamic-operation and process-safety coverage.

## Before any public release

- Run the Tier-0 scaffold audit and reconcile every finding.
- Complete rights and attribution review for each redistributed payload,
  including mixed-source JSON, the FFDB data selection and retained historical imports.
  Catalogue references do not imply permission to distribute document bodies.
- Confirm the repository's placement against the reactor-family repository
  standard.

## Explicitly not planned

Vendor ranking, capability scoring, and any presentation that would let a
company claim read as an independently established result.
